Upload a CSV file from your bank. We'll parse and auto-classify the transactions.

Supported CSV Formats

Most bank CSV exports work automatically. We look for columns like:

  • Date: Date, Posted, Transaction Date, Posting Date
  • Description: Description, Memo, Details, Narrative, Payee
  • Amount: Amount, or separate Debit/Credit columns
  • Reference: Reference, Check Number (optional)
