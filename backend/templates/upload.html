Accepted formats: CSV or PDF bank statements.

Supported Formats

CSV Files

Most bank CSV exports work automatically. We look for columns like:

  • Date: Date, Posted, Transaction Date, Posting Date
  • Description: Description, Memo, Details, Narrative, Payee
  • Amount: Amount, or separate Debit/Credit columns
  • Reference: Reference, Check Number (optional)

PDF Files

We extract transactions from PDF bank statements by finding lines with dates and amounts. Works best with:

  • Text-based PDFs (not scanned images)
  • Standard bank statement layouts with date, description, and amount columns
  • Statements from most major banks (Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, etc.)
